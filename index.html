Build your baseline

Baseline Farmer starts with one idea: your baseline is the amount of crypto exposure you want to maintain under normal conditions. It is a centerline, not a floor.

During entry, the same measurement is used before the position is fully established. The goal is not to blindly DCA on a calendar. It is to keep moving toward the target while using meaningful dips to improve the value you lock into the position.

This does not place trades. It establishes the capital/state model and gives you a relative framework for deciding when to deploy.

The Baseline Farmer loop

1. EstablishUse tactical DCA to build exposure toward the target.
2. MeasureContinuously compare actual exposure with the baseline.
3. FarmBelow target is rebuild territory; above target is harvest territory.
4. HarvestConvert excess into realized capital rather than trying to call the exact top.
5. RecycleUse internally generated profit to increase the baseline when appropriate.
6. RepeatThe entry behavior and maintenance behavior are the same strategy at different stages.
Training objective

Stop thinking primarily in calendar intervals or absolute price predictions. Think in terms of where the market is relative to the baseline you are trying to establish or maintain and how much capital remains available to exploit that deviation.

The entry phase is therefore not a temporary set of rules. It is the user's first practice of the same farming loop that will eventually maintain and grow the position.
THE PHILOSOPHY

Farm the path. Protect the baseline. Let the market pay you for waiting.

Baseline Farmer is built around a simple idea: you do not have to predict every move to think clearly about a market that moves above and below a level of exposure you actually want to own.

WAIT → GO → HARVEST → WAIT

Your strategy

The long cycle provides the reason to stay engaged with the asset class while leaving room for shorter cycles of movement along the way. But the strategy does not require sitting idle for four or five years waiting for an all-time high.

Instead, you establish a baseline — the amount of crypto exposure you actually want — and farm meaningful movement around it. Below the baseline, weakness is an opportunity to rebuild. Above it, excess exposure is something you can harvest.

That turns the market's path into something useful instead of something you simply endure.

Why this is different from ordinary DCA

Calendar DCA treats every purchase opportunity as roughly equivalent. Baseline Farmer treats relative position as something worth observing. Capital can keep moving toward the target while tactical reserve remains available for genuine weakness.

The goal is not to buy the bottom. It is to avoid spending all your buying power before the market gives you a better opportunity — while also avoiding the opposite mistake of waiting forever for a perfect entry.

The intended result is a single mental model that works while the position is being built and after it is established.

What the tool actually does

It externalizes the things that are difficult to hold in your head: your target, current exposure, remaining capital, deviations, harvests, recycled profits and market context. It helps you compare today's situation with the baseline instead of making every decision from scratch.
What it deliberately does not do

It does not predict the future, identify the exact bottom or top, or turn a few indicators into a fake certainty score. Market data can describe a move without proving that the move is about to reverse.

The blind spot: reversals

A dip can keep falling. A rally can keep running. A market can look weak immediately before a violent reversal, or look strong immediately before it rolls over. Relative measurements are useful precisely because they tell you where you are; they do not tell you with certainty what happens next.

This matters most around major reversals and possible ATH behavior. The tool can show distance from ATH, recent range position, persistence, acceleration and other observable context, but it cannot expose every piece of information that a human may notice in price action, liquidity, news, sentiment or changing market structure. Your judgment remains part of the strategy.

The discipline is therefore not “trust the dashboard.” It is “use the dashboard so your judgment has a memory, a baseline and a capital plan behind it.”

WHAT THIS IS

A way to see one strategy philosophy

Baseline Farmer is a website built around one way of thinking about crypto exposure. The baseline, farming loop, tactical entry idea and capital ledger are the philosophy made visible and interactive.

It is not asking you to believe that the strategy is universally correct. It gives you a structure for exploring the idea: establish a target, measure where you are relative to it, preserve capital for meaningful opportunities, harvest excess when appropriate, and keep track of what happened.

No expertise required.

You do not need to be a professional trader, analyst, or financial expert to understand the framework. The website is intentionally showing the relationships in plain language so you can decide whether the way of thinking is useful to you.

The important words, in plain English

BaselineThe amount of crypto exposure you want to maintain as your normal target. It is a centerline, not a floor.
ExposureThe current dollar value of the crypto position being managed.
DeviationHow far current exposure is above or below the baseline.
Dip / rebuildExposure is below the baseline, so the framework treats the gap as something that may be rebuilt.
HarvestExposure is above the baseline, so the excess can be considered for realization rather than requiring an exact top call.
Tactical reserveCapital deliberately left available instead of being deployed immediately.
RecycleRealized internal profit is put back into the system to support a larger baseline.
TrajectoryA description of recent direction and market position—not a prediction of what happens next.
This is a strategy ideation tool, not financial advice.

Baseline Farmer does not promise returns, predict markets, recommend that anyone buy or sell anything, or claim that this philosophy will outperform another approach. It is an interactive expression of a strategy philosophy and topology: a way to explore how baseline-relative thinking might organize decisions and capital over time.

Markets can behave in ways the framework does not expose. A dip can become a larger decline; a rally can continue; reversals can happen without warning. The numbers on this site describe the framework and available market context. They do not remove uncertainty or replace your own judgment.

Use the tool to understand the idea—not as a promise about the future.

Prototype principle: externalize the strategy, capital state, and bookkeeping. Do not turn intuition into arbitrary indicator thresholds merely because software makes them possible.
