What this page is doing

Baseline Farmer manages a crypto position around a target amount of exposure. It looks for meaningful movement around that target, using price, volume, structure, and the market's recent path to help decide when to build, hold, or harvest rather than treating every move as a signal.

Your target tells us how much exposure you want. Market conditions tell us whether now is a sensible time to move toward it. Read the Market Strategy for the full model and how the pieces fit together.

Terms used here
BaselineThe amount of crypto exposure you want to maintain under normal conditions. It is a target, not a price floor.
Structural baselineA market reference built for a particular timeframe. Different timeframes can disagree, and that disagreement is useful.
Virtual baselineA hypothetical reference created by replaying the farming approach through historical data as if the position had already been funded.
DeviationHow far price or exposure has moved from its relevant reference.
Tactical reserveCapital held back so a meaningful move can be acted on instead of committing everything at once.
FOMOA late or extended buying environment. A strong rally alone is not FOMO; a recovery from a deep decline can mean something very different.
