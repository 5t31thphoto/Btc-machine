GUESSER LORE

So what is the Guesser actually doing?

In plain English: the Guesser looks at the market the way this whole tool does, makes a call, and lives with it on its own little account — the same baseline strategy you are using, running beside you. It does not get to rewrite history after the fact.

The important part: it is not trying to predict tomorrow's Bitcoin price. It is trying to get better at managing a Baseline Farmer position.

1. It looks at the field at several scales

The Guesser does not reduce the market to one number. Short and longer views can disagree, and that disagreement is useful.

Short viewWhat is happening right now?
Middle viewIs this a meaningful move or just a wiggle?
Longer viewWhat larger structure is this move happening inside?

2. It builds a reference for the farm

This is the weird part. The Guesser can pretend it entered before a stretch of history and then run the farming strategy through that history.

That gives it a virtual baseline: not a magic prediction line, but the reference created by the account it has been managing.

3. It watches price and participation

Volume does not simply mean “bullish.” The same surge in participation can mean very different things depending on where price came from and where it is now.

Near an all-time high, extension + acceleration + participation can make a rally look crowded. After a deep decline, strength and participation can instead support a recovery hypothesis.

4. It makes a farming decision

The output is deliberately familiar:

BUY · COREKeep making normal progress toward the baseline.
BUY · TACTICALUse reserved capital because the market is offering a more interesting deviation.
HOLD / PRESERVEDo not force the exposure gap closed when the market does not offer the right setup.
HARVESTTake excess value when the position and market context make that sensible.

5. The farmer grows with the farm

The Guesser has a little visual memory of how its farm is doing. It starts as a scrappy farmer. As its account succeeds and builds a stronger baseline, the character gradually gains technology until it becomes the holographic farmer you see in the Baseline Farmer logo.

If the farm takes a beating, that progress can go the other way. The Guesser gets more worn down instead of pretending every experiment is a success.

It is mostly for fun, but there is a useful idea underneath it: the character's appearance is tied to the history of the account it is managing.

5. Then it has to live with the result

Every Guesser decision can become a frozen training node. Later market observations reveal what happened after that decision.

The important distinction is that the Guesser grades its own account. Your real decisions are kept separately as journal history.

6. The thing that learns is the manager

When enough outcomes mature, the Guesser can replay the same path with small variations in how aggressively it buys, harvests, preserves capital, or recycles profit.

If a variation manages its account better, the policy can move toward it. Future decisions therefore start from what the earlier Guesser learned.

A few technical tidbits

  • Everything here runs locally in the page. No cloud model is required.
  • Historical decisions are evaluated forward through later observations rather than being judged only by whether price went up or down.
  • The virtual account tracks crypto, cash, extracted capital, recycled profit, and baseline growth.
  • The policy is adaptive rather than a fixed “if RSI then buy” recipe.
  • Each matured decision is scored with counterfactuals (what BUY / HOLD / HARVEST would have produced on the same path).
  • Linear action weights and a similarity memory both learn from those outcomes.
  • The system can keep several competing market interpretations alive: recovery/reversal, continuation, correction, and late-cycle extension.

What it still cannot know

The Guesser is sophisticated because it has a state, memory, its own account, historical consequences, and an adaptive management policy. That does not make it clairvoyant.

News, liquidity, regime changes, manipulation, and things a human notices in the tape can still surprise it. A good result should make the policy better—not turn uncertainty into fake certainty.

The Guesser is an experimental companion for baseline farming, not financial advice.
